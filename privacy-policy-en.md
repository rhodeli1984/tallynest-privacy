## 1. How We Collect and Use Your Personal Information

**Information Collected:**
- Via phone registration: phone number, verification code

**Purpose of Use:**
- Create and manage your account
- Verify user identity
- Provide account security

**Legal Basis:** Legitimate interest in providing and improving our services, performance of contract

**Information Collected:**
- Family member nickname or name (customizable, no real name required)

**Purpose of Use:**
- Establish family relationship network
- Assign and manage tasks
- Record member activities and achievements

**Important Notes:**
- Family member information is used only within your family
- We do not require real names; you may use nicknames

**Legal Basis:** Performance of contract, legitimate interest

### 1.3 Task Management Features

**Information Collected:**
- Task title, description, due date
- Task assignee
- Task completion status and time
- Task-related photos or files (optional)

**Purpose of Use:**
- Provide task creation, assignment, and tracking features
- Generate task statistics and reports
- Support celebration and positive encouragement mechanisms

**Legal Basis:** Performance of contract

### 1.4 Device Information and Logs

**Information Collected:**
- Device model, operating system version
- Device unique identifiers (IDFA/Android ID, collected only with your authorization)
- IP address
- Application usage logs (crash logs, performance data)

**Purpose of Use:**
- Ensure proper application operation
- Identify and fix technical issues
- Analyze and improve product performance
- Security protection and fraud prevention

**Legal Basis:** Legitimate interest in maintaining and improving our services

### 1.5 Push Notifications

**Information Collected:**
- Device push token
- Notification preference settings

**Purpose of Use:**
- Send task reminders

- Send important system messages

**Important Note:** You can disable push notifications at any time in your system settings

**Legal Basis:** Consent, performance of contract

### 1.6 Payment Information

**Information Collected:**
- Order information (purchased items or services, amount, time)

**Important Notes:**
- Payment information is processed by third-party payment providers (WeChat Pay, Alipay, Stripe)
- We do not collect or store your bank card numbers, credit card numbers, or other sensitive payment information

**Purpose of Use:**
- Process your purchase requests
- Order management and customer service
- Fraud prevention

**Legal Basis:** Performance of contract, legal obligation

### 1.7 Camera and Photo Library Permissions (Optional)

**Information Collected:**
- Photos or videos you choose to upload

**Purpose of Use:**
- Set user or family member profile pictures
- Upload task-related images

**Important Notes:**

**Legal Basis:** Consent

## 2. How We Store and Protect Your Personal Information

### 2.1 Storage Location

Your personal information will be stored in the People's Republic of China. If cross-border data transfer is necessary, we will strictly comply with relevant provisions of Chinese data protection laws to ensure your information security.

### 2.2 Retention Period

- **Account Information:** Retained for the duration of your account existence
- **Task Data:** Deleted within 30 days after you actively delete it or close your account
- **Log Information:** Retained for no more than 6 months
- **Closed Account Information:** Immediately deleted or anonymized, unless otherwise required by law

### 2.3 Security Measures

We implement the following measures to protect your personal information:

1. **Data Encryption:** SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission, encryption for sensitive data storage
2. **Access Control:** Strict data access permission management, principle of least privilege
3. **Security Audits:** Regular security audits and vulnerability scanning
4. **Incident Response:** Established data security incident emergency response plan
5. **Employee Training:** Regular data security and privacy protection training for employees

### 2.4 Security Incident Notification

In the event of personal information leakage, tampering, loss, or other security incidents, we will:

1. Immediately activate emergency response plan to prevent incident escalation
2. Report to relevant regulatory authorities as required by law
3. Promptly notify you via push notification, email, or other means about the incident details, potential impact, measures we have taken, and risk prevention measures you can take

## 4. How You Can Manage Your Personal Information

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

### 4.1 Access and Copy Your Personal Information

You can access your personal information through the following paths:

- **Personal Profile:** "My" → "Personal Information"
- **Family Member Information:** "Family" → "Member Management"
- **Task Records:** "Tasks" → "History"

You can obtain a copy of your personal information by contacting us.

### 4.2 Correct Your Personal Information

If you find errors in the personal information we process, you have the right to request correction. You can edit directly within the app or contact us for corrections.

### 4.3 Delete Your Personal Information

You may request deletion of personal information in the following circumstances:

1. The processing purpose has been achieved, cannot be achieved, or is no longer necessary
2. We stop providing products or services
3. You withdraw consent
4. We process your personal information unlawfully

**Deletion Paths:**
- Delete a single task record: Long press task → "Delete"
- Delete family member: Enter member details → "Delete Member"

### 4.4 Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw your consent to our processing of personal information at any time:

- **Camera and Photo Library Permissions:** Disable through system settings
- **Push Notifications:** Disable through system settings or in-app settings
- **Third-Party Login Authorization:** Revoke authorization through third-party platform

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal.

### 4.5 Delete Account

You can request to delete your account through "My" → "Settings" → "Delete Account."

**Consequences of Account Deletion:**
- You will no longer be able to use this account
- All information under this account will be deleted and cannot be recovered
- You will not be able to retrieve any historical tasks or family data

### 4.6 Obtain Copy of Personal Information

You have the right to obtain a copy of your personal information. Please contact us using the contact information in the "How to Contact Us" section, and we will provide it within 15 business days.

### 4.7 Response to Your Requests

For security purposes, you may need to provide a written request or otherwise verify your identity. We will respond to your request within 15 business days.

If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may file complaints with regulatory authorities such as cyberspace administration, telecommunications, or public security departments.

## 6. Special Protection for Minors' Information

We attach great importance to the protection of minors' personal information.

### 6.1 Notice for Minors

- If you are a minor under 18 years of age, you should read this policy with your guardian and use our services only with your guardian's consent
- Guardians should guide minors in using this service and ensure minors understand the content of this policy

### 6.2 Adding Minor Information

- Adult users can add family member information in the application, including minors
- Minor information added does not require real names; nicknames may be used
- Minor information is used only within the family and will not be publicly disclosed or used for other commercial purposes

### 6.3 Guardian Rights

If you are a guardian of a minor, you have the right to:

1. Access, correct, and delete the minor's information
2. Refuse our further collection of the minor's information
3. Request that we delete the minor's account and information

If you discover that we have collected a minor's information without guardian consent, please contact us immediately, and we will delete it as soon as possible.
