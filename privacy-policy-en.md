# TallyNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

TallyNest helps parents manage family tasks, rewards, and points. This policy describes how the current version handles information you enter about yourself and your family. You may use the app as a guest or sign in with Apple. Please avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information in family names, child nicknames, or tasks.

## 1. Information We Handle

- **Sign-in information:** Sign in with Apple requests the name and email scopes. The app uses the app-specific user identifier Apple returns to identify your account and stores the name Apple returns, if any; the current version does not store the returned email address. Guest mode does not obtain a Sign in with Apple identifier.
- **Family and task data:** Family names, parent roles or preferred names, child nicknames and preset avatars, tasks and completion records, reward selections, point balances and transactions, and reminder and task/reward library preferences that you enter or select. Children may use nicknames rather than real names.
- **Notification permission:** If you enable review reminders, the app requests permission for iOS local notifications and schedules reminders on your device. The current version does not collect a remote push token for this feature.

The current version does not request location, contacts, camera, or photo access. It does not use advertising identifiers for tracking or include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not ask you for card or other payment details.

## 2. Storage, Guest Migration, and iCloud Sync

- Guest data and preferences stay in separate local storage on the current device and do not automatically sync across devices.
- When you first sign in with an Apple account, the app merges local guest data that has not already been assigned to another Apple account into that account's separate storage. It keeps the local guest copy. Repeated migration deduplicates records by identifier. Guest data already assigned to one Apple account is not automatically migrated to a different Apple account.
- For Apple accounts, family, child, task, completion, and point transaction data sync through Apple CloudKit to the private database of the iCloud account signed in on the device. Custom tasks, rewards, and related preferences sync through Apple's iCloud key-value storage. Cross-device sync requires the same Sign in with Apple identity and iCloud account on each device, available iCloud services, and a network connection. Offline changes remain local until the app can attempt sync; sync can be delayed or fail.
- Different Sign in with Apple identities use separate local stores. We do not operate a separate app cloud database. Apple stores iCloud data under its terms and privacy policy and determines the storage region.

## 3. Retention and Deletion

Data remains on the relevant device and, where applicable, in your private iCloud database until you delete records, remove the app, or manage the data through Apple's iCloud controls. Records deleted in the app are reflected in iCloud and other devices after successful sync; other devices may temporarily show older data while offline or when sync fails. Removing the app deletes its local data on that device but does not necessarily remove iCloud copies or data on other devices.

Confirming sign-out from the account row on the My screen ends the current app session only. It does not delete that account's local data, iCloud data, or the retained guest copy. The current version does not have an in-app one-tap account deletion feature. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact us at the address below. You can also edit or delete supported data in the app and manage cloud copies through Apple's iCloud settings. We will verify and handle requests under applicable law; we do not promise that Apple will remove iCloud data within a fixed number of days.

## 4. Sharing, Security, and Children's Information

Apple processes relevant information to provide Sign in with Apple and iCloud sync. We do not sell family data or provide it to third-party advertisers. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for its practices.

The local database uses iOS file protection. Apple provides platform protections for transfer to and storage in iCloud. No storage or transmission method is completely secure; please protect your device and Apple account.

The app is intended for parents. Parents decide whether to enter information about children and may use nicknames and preset avatars to limit identifying details. Children should not independently provide sensitive information. A parent or guardian should contact us about a child's data rights.

## 5. Your Choices and Policy Updates

You may use guest mode, decline local notification permission, edit or delete supported family data in the app, or sign out. Unavailable iCloud services may prevent cross-device sync for Apple accounts but do not prevent local use of data already on the device.

If our practices materially change, we will update this policy and its in-app version and provide an in-app notice where appropriate. Please review the latest version before continuing to use the app.

## 6. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests to access or delete data, email **privacy@tallynest.app**. Please do not email sensitive information about children. We may need to verify your control of the relevant account or device.
